2. Browser opens to http://localhost:8001/index.html
3. Navigate using the top menu

## Analyze Page (Strategy Generator)

### Step 1: Fill Business Information

**Required Fields:**

1. **Business Name**
   - Example: "Sweet Delights Bakery"

2. **Business Type**
   - Example: "Bakery", "Salon", "Restaurant"
   - Hint: Helps recommend right platforms

3. **Products/Services**
   - Example: "Custom cakes, pastries, desserts"
   - Be specific for better recommendations

4. **Location**
   - Example: "Downtown Chicago, IL"
   - Hint: Used for local marketing strategies

5. **Target Customers**
   - Example: "Families, young professionals, event planners"

6. **Price Range**
   - Budget-friendly (₹)
   - Mid-range (₹₹)
   - Premium (₹₹₹)

7. **Business Goals**
   - Example: "Increase local visibility, attract more customers"

**Optional Fields:**

8. **Current Digital Presence**
   - Example: "Facebook page, Instagram account"
   - Or "None" if just starting

### Step 2: Generate Strategy

1. Review all fields are filled
2. Click "Generate My Strategy" button
3. Loading spinner appears
4. Wait 2-5 seconds (AI processing)

#### 3. Growth Strategy

#### 4. Content Strategy

- **Posting Frequency** - How often to post on each platform

#### 5. Content Ideas

#### 6. Sample Content

#### 7. Local Expansion

## Using Generated Content

### Promotional Captions

1. **Copy** the caption you like
2. **Paste** into your social media platform
3. **Customize** if needed (add specifics)
4. **Add** the suggested hashtags
5. **Post** at optimal times

**Example:**
```
✨ Discover quality custom cakes at Sweet Delights! 
Visit us today and experience the difference. 📍

#Bakery #LocalBusiness #ShopLocal #CustomCakes
```

### Hashtags

1. **Use 8-15 hashtags** per post
2. **Mix** general (#LocalBusiness) and specific (#CustomCakes)
3. **Place** at end of caption or first comment
4. **Research** trending local hashtags

### Action Plan

1. **Print** or save the 30-day plan
2. **Follow** week by week
3. **Check off** completed tasks
4. **Adjust** based on your results
5. **Repeat** monthly with new strategies

## Common Questions

### How often should I generate a new strategy?

- **Monthly** - For ongoing optimization
- **Quarterly** - For major changes
- **When needed** - If goals or business changes

### Can I use the same captions multiple times?

- **No** - Each caption should be unique
- **Customize** - Adapt to current promotions/events
- **Regenerate** - Create new strategies for fresh content

### What if I don't have all the information?

- **Fill what you can** - System works with partial info
- **Use examples** - Look at similar businesses
- **Update later** - Generate new strategy when ready

### How do I know if it's working?

Track these metrics:
- **Followers** - Growing audience
- **Engagement** - Likes, comments, shares
- **Reach** - How many people see posts
- **Inquiries** - Messages and calls
- **Sales** - Actual business impact
